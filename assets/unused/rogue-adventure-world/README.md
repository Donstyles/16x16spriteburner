# Rogue Adventure World (ElvGames), kept for later

Tilesets and item icons from ElvGames' *Rogue Adventure World*, by the same artist as our cellar. The pack's enemies are
left out (tiny chibi sprites, less detailed than our creatures), as are the Godot/Unity copies and the alternative
icon layouts. Not used by the game yet: `tools/build.js` only embeds the files it names. All 16 px tiles.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `tilesets/` | **Underground, fits as is:** Crypt (stone, moss, blood, bones, candles), Cavern, Hell (bones, skulls, lava), Graveyard, Ruins, Lost City, Ancient (recolour its glowing green glyphs, which read as sci-fi). **Needs toning down:** Beast (walls of flesh and teeth; neon red as delivered), Sanctuary, Temple (shift its pink to stone). **Doesn't suit a dungeon:** Atlantis (glowing teal panels, looks like a spaceship), Pyramid (drawn in perspective). **Above ground**, for a village or overworld: Overworld, Village, Jungle, Wasteland, Ship, Interior. `*_Animations`/`*_Doors` sheets belong to the set they are named after |
| `item-icons/` | `RA_Item_Icons01–03`: swords, bows, staves, axes, helmets, armour, shields, gloves, boots, each in several metals |
| `trees/` | Animated trees in three colours |
